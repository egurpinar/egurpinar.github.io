Hello there and welcome to my home page! I am a power electronics engineer focused on developing high performance power electronic systems for sustainable transportation application. 

I received the B.Sc. degree from [Istanbul Technical University](https://www.itu.edu.tr/en/homepage), Istanbul, Turkey, in 2009, the M.Sc. degree from the [University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk), Manchester, U.K., in 2010, and the Ph.D. degree from the [University of Nottingham](https://www.nottingham.ac.uk), Nottingham, U.K., in 2017, all in electrical engineering. 

Between 2010 and 2014, I worked at [University of Reading](https://www.reading.ac.uk), UK and [GE Power Conversion](https://www.gevernova.com/power-conversion), UK as R&D Power Electronics Engineer respectively. Between May 2017 and March 2022, I was a member of Power Electronics and Electric Machinery Research Group, [Oak Ridge National Laboratory](https://www.ornl.gov) Knoxville, TN, USA, as a Postdoctoral Research Associate and a R&D Staff. In March 2022, I joined [Sikorsky Innovations](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/capabilities/sikorsky/sikorsky-innovations.html), Sikorsky Aircraft Corporation, Stratford, CT, USA as Senior Staff. 

My research interests include wide-bandgap power devices, high frequency converters, packaging and integration of power electronic systems, and electrified transportation including electric vertical take-off and landing vehicles (eVTOLs).
