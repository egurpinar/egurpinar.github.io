At Sikorsky, I have been leading power electronics system development for the Hybrid Electric Demonstrator (HEX), which is a a 9,000-pound maximum gross weight aircraft with 1.2MW-class turbogenerator and associated power electronics. My focus is on design, analysis and testing of aerospace grade electronics that can be used for electrified propulsion systems. 

You can find more information about the project in the [press release](https://news.lockheedmartin.com/2024-02-27-Sikorsky-Looks-to-Future-Family-of-VTOL-Systems).
