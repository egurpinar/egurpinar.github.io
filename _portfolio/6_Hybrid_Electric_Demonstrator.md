I am leading power electronics system development for the Hybrid Electric Demonstrator (HEX), which is a a 9,000-pound maximum gross weight aircraft with 1.2MW-class turbogenerator and associated power electronics. You can find more information about the project in the [press release](https://news.lockheedmartin.com/2024-02-27-Sikorsky-Looks-to-Future-Family-of-VTOL-Systems).
